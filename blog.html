Engineering FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to the most common inquiries regarding mold build times, resin availability, tolerances, and quality verification.

What are standard lead times for prototype vs. production tooling?

For rapid prototype tooling (SPI Class 104 aluminum or P20 steel), our typical lead time is 10 to 15 business days with First Article samples shipped immediately. For hardened SPI Class 101 production molds (multi-cavity hot runner in H13 or S7 steel), lead times range from 4 to 7 weeks depending on part complexity, slides, and lifters.

What tolerances can Nexus Plastics consistently hold?

Our standard commercial tolerance is ±0.004 in (±0.10 mm). For critical precision features using low-shrinkage engineering resins (e.g., Delrin, PEEK, or glass-filled PC), our electric ARBURG press fleet regularly holds ±0.0015 in (±0.038 mm), continuously verified via automated optical Zeiss CMM inspection.

How does your engineering team handle DFM and Moldflow analysis?

Every incoming CAD submission undergoes a complimentary 30-point DFM audit covering wall thickness uniformity, draft adequacy, sink risk, and gate placement. We execute full Autodesk Moldflow simulations to predict fill times, fiber orientation, and cooling warpage before any tool steel is cut.

Do you provide full PPAP Level 3 documentation for automotive and medical clients?

Yes. Under our IATF 16949 and ISO 13485 certifications, we generate complete PPAP packages including Design and Process FMEA, Control Plans, Gage R&R studies, First Article Inspection reports (FAI), and material composition certifications (IMDS / RoHS / REACH).

Can customer-owned existing molds be transferred into your facility?

We have a dedicated tooling transfer onboarding process. Our master toolmakers disassemble, inspect, clean, and verify tool condition, establish sensor baselines, and sample parts on our presses with zero downtime transition for ongoing OEM production contracts.
