Engineering Best Practices

DFM Quick-Reference Guidelines

Incorporate these critical injection molding design rules into your CAD models to prevent sink marks, reduce cycle times, and avoid costly mold revisions.

0.5° - 2.0°

Draft Angle per Side

Provide minimum 1° draft for polished surfaces and 1.5°–3.0° for textured (VDI / SPI) finishes to allow clean ejection without drag marks.

40% - 60%

Rib-to-Wall Ratio

Maintain internal structural rib thickness at 40% to 60% of adjacent nominal wall thickness to eliminate surface sink marks.

± 15% Max

Wall Thickness Uniformity

Keep nominal wall thicknesses uniform throughout the part. Blend thick-to-thin transitions with gradual 3:1 chamfers or radii.

R = 0.5 × T

Internal Corner Radii

Apply inside radii equal to 50% of wall thickness (T) to eliminate stress concentrations, reduce knit-line vulnerability, and improve flow.
