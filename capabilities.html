Comprehensive
Manufacturing Capabilities

From initial concept and tool design to high-volume injection molding and secondary assembly. We bring precision to every stage.

Precision Injection Molding

Our core expertise lies in high-precision, tight-tolerance injection molding. We utilize state-of-the-art electric and hybrid presses ranging from 50 to 1,500 tons, capable of producing components weighing from less than a gram to over 10 pounds.

  • High-volume automated production
  • Overmolding and Insert Molding
  • Two-Shot (2K) Multi-Material Molding

In-House Tooling & Mold Making

A great part starts with a great mold. Our fully equipped in-house tool room allows us to design, fabricate, maintain, and repair complex steel and aluminum molds, ensuring maximum uptime and component quality.

  • Class 101 Production Tooling
  • CNC Machining & Wire EDM
  • Preventative Mold Maintenance

Quality Assurance & Metrology

We operate under a strict ISO 13485 certified quality management system. Our climate-controlled metrology lab is equipped with advanced CMMs, vision systems, and spectroscopy equipment to guarantee your parts meet every specification.

  • First Article Inspection (FAI)
  • Statistical Process Control (SPC)
  • Full Material Traceability
