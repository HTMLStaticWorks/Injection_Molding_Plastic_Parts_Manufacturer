Process Architecture

The Injection Molding Cycle

01

Plastication

Raw resin pellets are fed into the heated barrel, sheared by the reciprocating screw, and melted into a homogeneous polymer pool.

02

Injection

The screw is pushed forward under high pressure, injecting the molten plastic into the clamped mold cavity at precise velocities.

03

Cooling Phase

Water or oil circulates through internal mold channels, drawing heat away to solidify the plastic while maintaining dimensional stability.

04

Ejection & QC

The mold opens and ejector pins release the finished part, which is then removed by robotics for automated vision inspection.
