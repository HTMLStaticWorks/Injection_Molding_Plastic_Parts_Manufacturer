Core Capabilities

Comprehensive Plastic Solutions

From initial concept to final assembled product, we provide a full spectrum of manufacturing services under one roof.

Custom Injection Molding

High-precision molding for complex geometries. Tonnage ranging from 50 to 1,500 tons supporting both small intricate parts and large housing enclosures.

Tooling & Mold Development

In-house mold engineering and CNC machining. We build SPI Class 101 production molds designed for millions of cycles with guaranteed maintenance.

Insert & Overmolding

Multi-material molding techniques to combine soft-touch TPEs with rigid substrates or encapsulate metal inserts for enhanced strength and functionality.

Rapid Prototyping

Bridge tooling and 3D printing services to validate designs and test functionality before committing to expensive production steel tooling.

Assembly & Secondary Ops

Ultrasonic welding, pad printing, heat staking, and full electro-mechanical assembly line services to deliver complete, ready-to-ship products.

Quality Assurance

Rigorous ISO-certified quality control including SPC tracking, FAI reporting, and advanced vision systems to ensure zero-defect manufacturing.
