Engineering Excellence in Plastics

Founded on principles of precision engineering, Nexus Plastics is a trusted partner for OEMs requiring demanding tolerances and complex geometries.

More Than Just a Molder

For over two decades, we have pushed the boundaries of plastic manufacturing. We operate a 150,000 sq ft state-of-the-art facility dedicated to comprehensive tool building and high-volume injection molding.

Our team of plastics engineers, toolmakers, and processing technicians collaborate directly with client design teams to optimize part designs for moldability, significantly reducing time-to-market and tooling costs.

  • End-to-End Solutions: From rapid prototyping to final assembly and custom packaging.
  • Advanced Materials: Deep expertise in processing high-performance engineering resins like PEEK, PEI, and PPS.
  • Global Logistics: Streamlined supply chain management serving critical industries worldwide.

Our Evolution

25 Years of Engineering Innovation

From a 3-press tool shop to an international tier-1 precision injection molding manufacturing powerhouse.

2001

Company Founded in Detroit

Established with 3 hydraulic presses and an in-house tool & die maintenance bench, delivering precision gears and brackets for regional automotive assemblers.

2008

ISO 9001 Certification & Toolroom Expansion

Formally accredited under ISO 9001 and invested $4M in Makino high-speed 5-axis CNC machining centers and Charmilles Wire EDM systems for internal SPI Class 101 tool fabrication.

2016

All-Electric Press Fleet & RJG Scientific Molding

Transitioned primary molding floor to all-electric ARBURG and Borche presses. Fully integrated RJG eDART cavity pressure sensors for automated bad-part rejection at the gate.

2021

ISO 13485 (Medical) & Class 8 Cleanroom Commissioning

Completed cleanroom construction for microfluidic, surgical cartridge, and diagnostic pipette molding under stringent bio-burden and particulate protocols.

2026
Current Benchmark

Industry 4.0 Smart Factory & AI Metrology

Operating 45 fully connected robotic injection molding cells with live cloud telemetry, automated automated guided vehicles (AGVs), and Zeiss optical 3D scan verification.
